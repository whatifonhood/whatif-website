# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not
the person writing it.

## What that means here

**Welcome:** disagreement, strong opinions about implementation, saying "this
is wrong and here is why", asking a question you think is obvious, being new.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment, sexual attention, discrimination
on any grounds, publishing someone's private information, or persistently
derailing a discussion after being asked to stop.

One more, because of what this project is: **no financial advice, no price
talk, and no shilling** in issues or pull requests. This repository is a
website. Nobody here is telling anyone to buy anything, and a discussion that
turns into one gets closed.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and anywhere else the
project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report anything to **[redacted]**. Reports are handled
privately and the reporter's identity is not shared.

I will read it, decide, and act — a warning, a deleted comment, or a ban,
depending on what happened. I will tell you what I decided. I am one person
maintaining a memecoin website, not a committee: expect a fair hearing and a
plain answer, not a formal process.

Maintainers who do not hold to this are subject to it too.
